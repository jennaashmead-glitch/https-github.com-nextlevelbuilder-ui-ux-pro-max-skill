Who We Are

A Decade of Doing It Right

Print Lab Wraps was founded on a simple idea: that every business, regardless of size, deserves branding that looks like it cost ten times more. We started as a two-person operation in a 1,200 sq ft garage. A decade later, we operate out of a 12,000 sq ft climate-controlled facility with a team of 20+ craftspeople who share that original obsession.

We've wrapped everything from single-vehicle personal projects to 200-truck fleet rollouts. We've branded storefronts, office lobbies, trade show booths, construction fences, and 10-story building exteriors. No job is too big, too small, or too complex — if it needs a wrap, we have a plan.
