Vehicle Wraps

Vehicle wrap pricing depends on the size of your vehicle, the complexity of the design, and the type of wrap (full, partial, or spot graphics). A standard full wrap for a passenger car typically ranges from $2,500 to $5,000, while larger vehicles like vans or trucks can range from $3,500 to $7,000+. We provide free estimates — contact us for an exact quote tailored to your vehicle and goals.

With proper care, a professionally installed vinyl wrap using premium 3M or Avery Dennison cast vinyl will last 5 to 7 years. Longevity depends on how the vehicle is stored (garaged vs. outdoor), how often it's washed, and exposure to harsh weather. Keeping your vehicle out of direct sunlight when possible can significantly extend the wrap's life.

No — in fact, wraps protect your paint. High-quality cast vinyl acts as a barrier against UV rays, minor road debris, and everyday scratches. When professionally removed, the original paint underneath is preserved. We always recommend a paint inspection prior to wrapping to confirm the surface is in good condition for adhesion.

A standard passenger car full wrap typically takes 1 to 2 business days. Larger vehicles like vans, trucks, and trailers may take 2 to 4 days depending on complexity. Custom cuts, specialty finishes, and multi-piece installations may require additional time. We'll give you a firm turnaround estimate with your quote.

Commercial Fleets

Yes — fleet pricing is one of our specialties. We offer tiered volume discounts starting at 3 or more vehicles. The more vehicles in your program, the greater the savings per unit. We also offer phased fleet programs if you're rolling out branding across a large fleet over time. Contact us to discuss fleet pricing for your specific needs.

Fleet turnaround depends on the number of vehicles and design complexity. For smaller fleets of 5–10 vehicles, we typically complete the project within 2–3 weeks from design approval. Larger programs are scheduled in phases to minimize downtime — we work around your operational schedule to keep your vehicles on the road.

Absolutely. Our in-house design team specializes in fleet branding that creates a cohesive, professional look across every vehicle. We work from your existing brand guidelines or can help develop a fleet identity from scratch. Design fees vary by scope, and design costs are often included in larger fleet packages.

Signage & Prints

We use a range of professional-grade substrates depending on the application: coroplast and aluminum for yard signs and A-frames, PVC and aluminum composite panels for wall-mounted signs, vinyl banner material with reinforced hems and grommets for banners, and specialty films for window graphics. All prints use UV-resistant inks for lasting color.

Outdoor durability depends on the material and environmental conditions. Vinyl banners typically last 2–5 years. Aluminum and PVC panel signage can last 7–10+ years with proper installation. Window graphics using exterior-grade vinyl last 3–5 years. We'll recommend the right material for your specific outdoor application and expected lifespan requirements.

Most standard print orders — banners, yard signs, window decals — are ready within 3–5 business days after artwork is approved. Rush orders (1–2 business days) are available for an additional fee, subject to production capacity. Large or complex orders, like trade show displays or full wall murals, may require 7–10 business days.

General

We back all of our wrap installations with a craftsmanship warranty. Our standard warranty covers installation defects — lifting edges, bubbling, or peeling — for 1 year on vehicle wraps and 6 months on signage applications. Material warranties are provided by the manufacturer (3M, Avery Dennison) and typically cover 3–7 years depending on the product line. We'll review specifics with you at the time of your project.

We serve clients throughout the greater metro area and surrounding region. We also accommodate customers who travel to our facility from further distances for specialty projects. For large fleet programs, we can discuss on-site or regional installation arrangements. Contact us to confirm service availability for your location.

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), check, ACH bank transfer, and cash. For commercial projects over $1,000, we typically require a 50% deposit to begin design and production, with the balance due upon project completion. Fleet programs may be invoiced on net-30 terms for approved business accounts.
