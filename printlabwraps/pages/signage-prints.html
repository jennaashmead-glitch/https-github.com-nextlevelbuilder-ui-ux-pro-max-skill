Durability & Quality

Built to Last Outdoors

Not all signage is created equal. At Print Lab Wraps, every outdoor print job uses UV-resistant inks that resist fading, cracking, and color shift even under constant sun exposure. Our laminates seal the surface against moisture, dirt, and abrasion.

We match the right substrate to your application — heavy-duty 13oz scrim vinyl for banners, 3mm coroplast or aluminum composite for signs, and specialty films for window and surface applications. Every job comes with material recommendations based on your environment and expected lifespan.

UV-resistant inks maintain vibrant color for 3–7+ years outdoors
Laminate coatings protect against moisture, scuffs, and UV degradation
Substrate options matched to application — vinyl, aluminum, coroplast, PVC
Rush orders available — most standard prints ready in 3–5 business days

Our Process

Fast, Simple, Done Right

01

Submit Your Project

Tell us what you need — dimensions, quantity, material preferences, and intended use. We'll confirm specs and provide an accurate quote fast.

02

Artwork & Proofing

Submit your ready-to-print files or work with our design team. We provide a digital proof for approval before production begins — no surprises.

03

Print & Finish

Your job runs on our large-format press with UV inks, then laminated, trimmed, and finished to exact specifications — ready for real-world performance.

04

Pick up from our shop or arrange delivery. We also offer professional installation services for large signage, wall graphics, and window film applications.
