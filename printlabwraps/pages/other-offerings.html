Specialty Vehicles

We Wrap Everything

Boat Wraps

Custom boat wraps for fishing boats, bass boats, ski boats, pontoons, and center consoles. We work with fiberglass, aluminum, and composite hulls.

Trailer Wraps

Full and partial wraps for enclosed cargo trailers, open utility trailers, horse trailers, and specialty haulers. Ideal for business branding on the move.

Golf Cart Wraps

Golf cart wraps for personal carts, resort fleets, event transportation, and community vehicles. Fun graphics or clean branded finishes — your call.

ATV & UTV Wraps

Wraps for ATVs, UTVs, side-by-sides, and off-road vehicles. Built tough with outdoor-rated vinyl that handles mud, UV exposure, and rough terrain.

Snowmobile Wraps

Bold custom graphics for snowmobiles and sleds. Stand out on the trail with wrap designs built for cold-weather adhesion and long-term performance.

Custom Specialty Projects

Have something unusual? We love a challenge. From parade floats to food trucks, kiosks to aircraft interiors — if it has a surface, we have a solution.

The Most Creative Wraps We Do

Specialty wraps are where our team's creativity truly shines. These projects often require custom templates, unique material selections, and installation techniques we've developed over years of tackling non-standard surfaces. No two specialty projects are exactly the same — and that's exactly how we like it.

Our Process

We Make This Easy.

01

Consultation

We start by understanding your vehicle type, surface conditions, and vision. Specialty wraps often need unique templates — we handle all of it.

02

Design

We create custom templates specific to your vehicle's exact geometry, ensuring the design fits perfectly around every curve and cutout.

03

Production

Printed on outdoor-rated cast vinyl selected for your vehicle's environment — marine-grade for boats, cold-rated for snowmobiles, UV-rated for all.

04

Installation

Our specialist installers handle surfaces that standard shops won't touch. Complex curves, riveted panels, fiberglass — we've done it all.
