Anything & Everything

If It Has a Surface,
We Can Wrap It

Most wrap shops stop at cars and trucks. At Print Lab Wraps, we take on the projects others won't. Our installers have experience working on curved hulls, textured plastic panels, fiberglass bodies, and irregular surfaces that demand skill and patience beyond standard vehicle work.

We use the right vinyl for the right application — marine-grade for watercraft, cast vinyl for complex contours, and overlaminate films for surfaces that see abrasion or UV. Before we start, we consult with you on the best material approach for your specific project and environment.

Have something truly unique? We love a challenge. Reach out and describe your project — no idea is too unusual for an initial consultation.

How It Works

Simple Process, Exceptional Results

01

Describe Your Project

Tell us what you're wrapping, your color or graphic vision, and how the item is used. We'll assess the right approach and provide an honest, detailed estimate.

02

Design Preview

For custom graphic projects, our design team builds a digital mockup so you can visualize the finished product before a single panel is printed. Revisions are included.

03

Expert Installation

We print, prep, and install using techniques and adhesives matched to your specific material and surface. Specialty projects get extra hands and extra care.

04

Ready to Turn Heads

We do a final inspection, provide care instructions specific to your item, and send you on your way with a wrap that commands attention wherever it goes.
